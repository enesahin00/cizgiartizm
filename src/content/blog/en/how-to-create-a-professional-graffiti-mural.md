A professional graffiti mural is about much more than paint and spray cans; it
is a process that requires thorough preparation, accurate scaling and the right
choice of materials. Here is a step-by-step guide.

## 1. Preparing the Wall

A good result starts with a solid surface. Loose plaster is removed, damage is
repaired, the wall is damp-proofed and a suitable primer is applied. Skip this
step and even the most beautiful design will deteriorate over time.

## 2. Scaling the Design

The approved design is transferred to the wall in the correct proportions,
using either a projector or the classic grid technique. Accurate scaling makes
sure the artwork looks proportional from every angle.

## 3. Choosing the Materials

- **Outdoors:** UV- and weather-resistant acrylic and spray paints
- **Indoors:** Low-odor, water-based acrylic paints

The type of surface (concrete, wood, metal) and the location of the space
determine which materials are used.

## 4. Painting and Protection

The work is built up in layers, with the details saved for last. The finished
piece can be sealed with a protective varnish to extend its life.

A mural made by a professional team with experience and the right materials
keeps its first-day vibrancy for years.
