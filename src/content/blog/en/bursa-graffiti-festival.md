Organized by Bursa Metropolitan Municipality at Hüdavendigar City Park, the
festival became one of the biggest graffiti events in Türkiye.

Sixty-four artists from all over Türkiye and abroad worked non-stop for two
days. Over the course of the event the panels turned into a "giant canvas," and
the finished works were then taken on a traveling exhibition across Bursa.

Festivals like this bring street art to a wide audience and inspire young
artists. At Çizgi Artizm, we are proud to be part of this energy.
